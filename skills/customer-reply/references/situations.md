# Reply patterns — 12 situations small businesses get every day

Fill every [bracket] from FACTS.md. Rewrite in the brand voice. Never send a bracket.

1. **Price / menu** — "Here's our [menu/catalog link]. [Item] is [price]. Anything you'd like me to explain?"
2. **Opening hours** — "We're open [hours] — see you soon!"
3. **Location** — "We're at [address]. Map: [map link]."
4. **Booking / reservation** — "Happy to! What time and how many people? I'll hold it for you."
5. **Availability / stock** — If FACTS has it: state it. If not: "Let me check right now and come back to you in a few minutes."
6. **Delivery / shipping** — State the exact areas, fee and time from FACTS. Outside the area → offer pickup/visit.
7. **Payment methods** — List the methods from FACTS; share the payment link if listed.
8. **"I'll think about it"** — "No rush at all. Here's [link/photo] so it's easy to decide later. Message me anytime."
9. **"Somewhere else is cheaper"** — Don't attack the competitor. Restate one concrete thing you include (from FACTS), then offer the next step.
10. **Not tech-savvy / confused** — "No problem — just send me [the one thing you need] and I'll take care of the rest."
11. **Complaint / problem** — Apologise once, don't argue, don't promise compensation not in FACTS; use the hand-off line and flag to owner.
12. **Silent customer follow-up (2–3 days later)** — "Hi again! Just checking — did you get a chance to look at [thing]? Happy to help if anything's unclear." Send once only.

## Industry tweaks
- **Café / restaurant:** always offer to hold a table or share the map.
- **Salon / spa:** ask for preferred time + service; mention deposit rule if FACTS has one.
- **Repair shop:** ask for device model + a photo of the issue before quoting; never quote a repair price not in FACTS.
- **Online shop:** confirm size/variant, then share the order/payment link.
- **Service business (web, design, coaching):** offer a preview/sample or a short call as the next step.
